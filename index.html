Our friend Chris Porter was larger than life, and he left us 10 years ago. Today his family, friends, and bandmates celebrate him the best way we know how — with music.

Sunday, October 25 · 1:30–4:30 PM · Elysian Gardens

Today we share his music and photos.

Songs for Porter is also a fundraiser for Camp Cosby. Chris was a camper and counselor at Camp Cosby for many years. A donation of any size helps send a kid to camp who couldn’t otherwise afford to go. Small gifts are exactly what today is built on.

Prefer cash or check? Both work at the door — ask any volunteer.

About Camp Cosby

Camp Cosby is a YMCA sleepaway camp on Lake Logan Martin in Alpine, Alabama — 135 acres where kids spend a week outdoors, electronics-free. It’s where Chris was a camper, and later a counselor, for many years.

Every donation today goes to Camp Cosby’s Annual Campaign, which provides financial assistance for qualifying families — so a kid who deserves a week at camp can go, even if their family can’t afford it.
